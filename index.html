What I actually do

I'm a Data Analyst on Abbott's Global Strategies & Regulations team in Chicago, where I support ATLAS — an analytics program that turns regulatory reporting across 17 country markets into something a DVP can read in one glance instead of fifteen. That means KPI frameworks, executive Power BI dashboards, and more DAX than I'd like to admit to out loud.

My last recommendation wasn't a new dashboard — it was a glossary. The fastest way to stop the same question landing in your inbox for the fifth time is to put the answer where people are already looking.
Power BI DAX Python SQL dbt Looker Azure

Before Abbott, I was on contract with Techno Talent Inc., maintaining dashboards for clients who needed the numbers right, not just on time — good practice for a job that's mostly about making sure the number on the screen matches the number in reality.
